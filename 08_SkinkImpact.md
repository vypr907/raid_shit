### Acquisition

User said on December 4, 2025:

> “I just unlocked the new Predator character”

They also unlocked Xenomorph and a full set of 6★ Instinct gear.

### Kit supplied by user

The user explicitly provided Predator's full skill set:

**Wrist Blades**

* Attacks one enemy.
* Heals Predator for 30% of damage dealt.
* Passive counterattack whenever Predator successfully evades an enemy skill/effects.
* Damage based on HP.

**Smart Disc**

* Attacks all enemies twice.
* Initial target receives increased damage.
* Subsequent targets receive modified bonus damage.
* Destroys each target's MAX HP by 30% of damage dealt, stacking to 50%.
* Second hit has different bonus-damage progression.
* Ignores 15% DEF when Predator is under Veil/Perfect Veil.
* Damage based on HP.

**Combistick Throw**

* Attacks one enemy.
* Places Hunter's Gaze.
* Under Veil/Perfect Veil, Hunter's Gaze cannot be resisted.
* If it kills: Block Revive + Hunter's Gaze on another enemy.
* If it fails to kill: Perfect Veil for 2 turns + 50% TM fill.
* Damage based on HP.

**Yautja Cloak passive**

* Starts each round with Perfect Veil for 1 turn.
* Initial 50% chance to evade enemy skills before first turn.
* Initial evasion chance drops to 0 after first turn.
* Killing enemies grants additional 20% evasion, stacking to 60%.

**Aura**

* +35% Ally HP in Arena.

**Faction**

* User explicitly corrected the assistant: Predator is a **Legendary Lizardmen** champion.
