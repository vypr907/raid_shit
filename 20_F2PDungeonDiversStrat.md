# 1. CONVERSATION OVERVIEW

**Title:** RAID Early/Mid-Game Dungeon, Arena & Banner Lords Faction Wars Optimization

**Approximate date range:** September 8–16, 2025, with the conversation context later resurfacing on September 1, 2026.

**Primary purpose:**
This conversation focused on optimizing an early/mid-game F2P RAID: Shadow Legends account using the user's existing roster and relatively limited resources. The main areas were:

* Arena team composition and stat targets
* Fire Knight Turn Attack tournament strategy
* Spider team composition
* Dragon's Lair Turn Attack strategy
* Ice Golem event strategy
* Banner Lords Faction Wars team construction
* Gear/build recommendations
* Blessings for High Khatun and Michelangelo
* Gear and investment priorities for Banner Lords champions
* Event/tournament resource allocation

A recurring theme was **resource efficiency**: the user wanted to get the most progress possible from limited energy, silver, gear, and developed champions.

**Important correction established by the user:**
The assistant repeatedly misunderstood Turn Attack scoring. The user explicitly corrected that **lower turn counts produce more points**. This should be retained as a hard constraint when interpreting the historical discussion.

**Most important conclusions reached:**

1. The user's strongest developed core at the time was **Alice the Wanderer, Kael, Runekeeper Dazdurk, Ronda, and Fellhound**.
2. For several dungeon events, the recommendation was to work with this already-developed core rather than prematurely investing in poorly developed Banner Lords champions.
3. **Ronda had six Savage pieces at level 16**, making her a major damage-dealer candidate.
4. The user's Ice Golem progression was approximately **Stage 14**, but the Stage 14 clear was extremely fragile: **only Alice survived**.
5. For Ice Golem, the practical recommendation was to farm around **Stage 13–14** rather than forcing Stage 16+ until survivability improved.
6. The Banner Lords Faction Wars roster included **Ronda, Michelangelo, Lady Annabelle, Oathbound, Lordly Legionary, Steadfast Marshal, Commander, Archer, Frontline Warrior, Swordsman, Misericord, Riscarm, Denid the Tusk Knight, Quaestor, Bombardier, Courtier, Myrmidon, Fireblade, and Valerie**.
7. The proposed long-term Banner Lords FW team was **Michelangelo + Ronda + Oathbound + Lady Annabelle + Valerie**, although this was a **RECOMMENDED/PLANNED** composition, not a confirmed built/tested team.
8. The proposed Banner Lords investment order was **Michelangelo → Ronda → Oathbound → Lady Annabelle → Valerie**, but several of the underlying kit claims made by the assistant require verification before being treated as reliable RAID knowledge.
9. **Valerie was added later** and was considered as an alternative support option in Banner Lords FW.
10. High Khatun was recommended historically for **Cruelty**, with **Commanding Presence** as an alternative; Michelangelo was given **Brimstone** / **Nature's Wrath** alternatives. These recommendations were not confirmed as actually selected.
