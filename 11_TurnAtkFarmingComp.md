The conversation focused on optimizing dungeon teams specifically for **Turn Attack events**, where the user's priority is:

> **Highest possible dungeon tier while requiring the fewest possible turns.**

* Dragon's Lair Turn Attack stage selection

1. **Turn count, not energy efficiency, is the user's primary optimization metric.**

3. **Ezio was recommended as the user's best single-target DPS option**, but this was later qualified by the fact that his actual gear was inadequate.
4. The user explicitly decided **not to use Ezio in the Fire Knight team because he was not geared sufficiently well**.
5. The recommended Fire Knight team became:
   **Ronda / Fellhound / Kael / Alice / Predator**
6. The conversation attempted to create Round 1/2/3 AI presets for Fire Knight.
7. The user clarified that the game only provides **Round 1, Round 2, Round 3 skill presets**, rather than conditional presets based on whether Fire Knight's shield is currently active.
8. Exact skill names were supplied by the user for **Kael, Alice, and Ronda**, because earlier responses had incorrectly/ambiguously described their abilities.
9. No Clan Boss discussion occurred in this conversation.

**Important archival warning:** Several of the assistant's RAID mechanics claims—particularly descriptions of individual skills, Fire Knight shield behavior, Fellhound's kit, and Ezio's mastery/build mechanics—were stated without verification and may be incorrect.
