# General Strategy Notes

Freeform space for account-wide strategy: shard pull plans, energy usage, event priorities, artifact farming targets, etc.

## Content Priority (standing ranking)
1. **Clan Boss** - by a wide margin
2. **Ice Golem's Peak**
3. **Fire Knight's Castle**
4. Everything else (Hydra, Doom Tower, Cursed City, Sintranos) - lower priority
5. **Arena** - explicitly low priority, "I really only do it for quest/events"

## Playstyle Preferences
- Almost everything needs to work on **Auto** - manual-only strategies are a hard no.
- Willing to rebuild a team, but only for a **substantial** improvement - not marginal tweaks.
- Wants a **strict, sequential roadmap** (do this → spend these resources → build this → don't build this → next milestone), not just a tier list.
- Likes the promo champions (Alice the Wanderer, Ronda, Predator, Ezio Auditore) but explicitly prioritizes account strength over keeping favorites in top teams.
- Wants recommendations grounded in the actual owned/built roster - not generic tier lists or "theoretically best" comps that assume champions the account doesn't have or hasn't developed.
- For tournaments, optimize for the actual scoring rule, not an assumed one - Turn Attack tournaments score on **fewest turns** (confirmed twice), a regular Dragon Tournament rewards **highest reliable stage farmed**, and Shredder's Wrath is its own separate dungeon (doesn't double-dip with Spider farming).

## Shard Strategy
- No dedicated shard-pull plan established yet in the historical record.
- Resource snapshot (Aug 28, 2026): 13 Ancient, 6 Void, 6 Primal, 4 Sacred shards.

## Artifact Farming Priorities
- No artifact/gear-export tool is available (RSL Helper and RSL X-Tender both lack this), so gear planning has to work from known champion stats rather than a full inventory.
- Ice Golem Stage 10 was found more time-efficient than Stage 13 for tournament/accessory farming.

## Event Priorities
- Champion Training Tournaments: prioritize rank-ups (a 6★ especially) as the main point source; farm 12-3 Brutal for XP + Silver with 1 farmer + 3 food champions; stockpile Mystery Shards and food pre-event; use only 1-3 brews to accelerate early levels, finish via campaign XP; **never spend Legendary Books purely for tournament points**; double-dip with Dungeon Divers / Artifact Enhancement / CvC when they overlap.
